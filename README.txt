I used to have a part-time job in a newsagents.

One week, the News of World had a photo of a WWII bomber on the surface of
the moon with a headline claiming that Hitler had fled (successfully) to the
moon at the end of the war.

The next week, they just had a picture of the moon.  No bomber.  The
headline, in a font intended to express shock, was "It's gone!".

----

Well, in this case too, it's gone!
